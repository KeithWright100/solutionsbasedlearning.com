Adapted IB past paper questions

Papers 1, 2 and 3 in numbered sets. Write your answer to each adapted IB question and the marking bot gives you a mark, what you did well, what is missing and your next step.

How to use this page

Choose a set, pick a question, write your answer and press Submit for Marking. You cannot draw here, so describe any diagram in words: label the axes, the curves, any shift and the area or gap you are showing. Paper 2 and Paper 3 questions use texts and data from the exam paper, so ask your teacher for the paper that goes with the set before you answer. Paper 1 and Paper 2 are taken by SL and HL students; Paper 3 is HL only.
