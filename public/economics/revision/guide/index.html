IB Economics Full Revision Guide

Condensed notes, diagrams, worked calculations, key terms, real-world examples and quizzes for the whole IB Economics course (SL and HL).

How to use this guide

This guide is not a substitute for your lessons in school. Use it to consolidate what you have learned, to check your understanding, and to prepare for IB-style questions. The guide follows the IB syllabus in topic-sized chunks. Every topic has its Learning Intention, Syllabus Statement and Success criteria, clear notes with the diagrams you need to draw, key terms, an exam-style question, a Test My Knowledge quiz and Spaced Retrieval.

HL marks content, diagrams and calculations that are for Higher Level only. SL students can skip these sections.
