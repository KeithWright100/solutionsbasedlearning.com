Danillion

Live

A reverse-trivia challenge: each prompt has lots of right answers, but the rarer your answer, the more points you score. Everyone names Brazil for the Equator; name Kiribati and you shoot up the mountain. You get 25 seconds and 3 tries per prompt, and after each one you see the rarest answers you could have given. Score 500 points to reach the summit of Everest, climbing through farmland, forest, alpine meadow, snow and the death zone.

2 versions · 2 levels + Daily · 35 prompts
  • Level 1: world places
  • Level 2: night climb, harder
  • ⭐ Daily: same 7 prompts for everyone, one go a day
  • Countries, capitals, peaks, rivers, seas
  • Relaxed mode: no timer
  • Also as a Mariana Trench dive

Live
