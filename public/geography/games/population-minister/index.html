👥 Population Minister 2025–2055

You are the minister responsible for population policy. Over three decades, choose responses to your country’s population structure and watch the pyramid change. Then swap countries — or compare with a group who ran the other one.

1 · Read the pyramidStudy the age structure, dependency ratio and fertility rate. What is the main challenge?
2 · Choose responsesSpend your policy budget each decade. Some responses work in one country and backfire in the other.
3 · Face eventsRecessions, pandemics, migration crises and elections will test your plan.
4 · EvaluateSee your 2055 pyramid, compare both countries and write an evaluative paragraph from your own data.
