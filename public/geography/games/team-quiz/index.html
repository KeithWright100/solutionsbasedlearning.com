Team Quiz: Guess the Place

Teams of three join on one phone or laptop each, choose a team name, then race to name the place from a photo and clues.

3 clues a roundA photo on its own, then two written clues that get easier. 20 seconds each.
Fast and first try10 points on the photo (7 after clue 2, 4 after clue 3), plus 1 point for every second left of the first 10.
Second chancesWrong first time? A correct later answer still scores 5. Three tries a round.
No student dataTeams only give a team name. Games are deleted after 3 hours.
