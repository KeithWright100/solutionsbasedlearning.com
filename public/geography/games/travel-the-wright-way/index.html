✈️ Travel the Wright Way

Geography is so much more than capitals and countries. The subject studies human and physical features, and the connections (inter-relationships) between the human and physical environment, and so much more. This game tests your knowledge, ability to use a variety of geographical skills, and illustrates the diversity found on Earth.

Start in Thailand and travel the world. At each destination, explore by canoe, camel, longboat and more, then label diagrams, read graphs, photos, interpret models etc. Answer questions to earn your passport stamp to the next amazing place.
