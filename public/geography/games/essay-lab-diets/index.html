Stage 1: Rebuild the model essay

The essay has been cut into pieces and shuffled. Drag the ⋮⋮ handle (or use the arrows) to put the pieces in order. Look for clues: signposting words (However, Therefore, For example, Overall), which place is being discussed, and how each paragraph builds on the last.

    Stage 2: Colour-code the 6 P’s

    Choose a colour, then click words to highlight them (on a computer you can also click and drag across a phrase). Click a highlighted word again with the same colour to remove it. Find the key evaluation statements, definitions and key terms, places, processes, possibilities and power (players and decision-makers).

    Dashed red outline = a key phrase you haven’t found yet. Wavy red line = a key phrase given the wrong colour. You need to find at least 70% of the key phrases to unlock Stage 3.
