KS3 Geography · Measuring and mapping development

Development Detectives

You are an analyst for a world development agency. Your case: how developed is a country, really? Your teacher will stop the class at each chapter. When you finish early, take on the challenge.

Learning intention
I can explain how development is measured and mapped, and judge how useful different indicators are.
Success criteria · I can:
  1. SC1 describe economic, social and composite indicators of development (e.g. GNI per capita, literacy rate, HDI)
  2. SC2 explain the strengths and weaknesses of each type of indicator
  3. SC3 evaluate how useful HDI is as a measure of development, and judge whether it is the best indicator
