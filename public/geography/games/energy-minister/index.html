🏛️ Energy Minister 2025–2050

You have just been appointed Energy Minister. Over three decades you must reshape your country’s electricity mix. Aim for a balanced energy mix that is sustainable, secure, reliable and affordable — and survive whatever the world throws at you.

1 · Build & retireUse your investment budget to build new capacity (+) and retire old power stations (−). Shares must add up to 100%.
2 · Cut demandEach decade, pick one sustainable energy management policy: efficiency, conservation or education.
3 · Face eventsA random event hits each decade — price shocks, droughts, storms, new technology. A balanced mix protects you.
4 · ReviewGet your 2050 scorecard, then use your data to write an exam-style paragraph.
