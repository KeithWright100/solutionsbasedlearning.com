Tropical Storm: Link It Up

In pairs, make as many links between the characteristics and impacts as you can. Tap a cause circle, then tap the impact circle it leads to, and explain the link.

Example: Education → Number of injuries/deaths — “Better education may lead to fewer deaths because people understand storm warnings and know how to respond safely.”
