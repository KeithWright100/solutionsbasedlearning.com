    Exam tip: a change in the good’s own price never shifts its own demand or supply curve — it causes a movement along the curve (an extension or contraction). Only the other factors (income, tastes, prices of related goods, costs, technology, taxes, subsidies, weather…) shift a curve. Examiners love this distinction.
