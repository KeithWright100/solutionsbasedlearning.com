Balance It!

The accountant has left a pile of figures on your desk. Build each business’s statement of financial position in the IB format, bin anything that does not belong on it, work out the subtotals, and make it balance.

1. Place the itemsTap an item card, then tap a gap in the statement (or drag it there). Each item belongs in one section: assets, liabilities or equity.
2. Bin the impostersExam papers mix in items from the statement of profit or loss, such as revenue, cost of sales or dividends. They go in the bin, not on the balance sheet.
3. Calculate and balanceFill in every subtotal. When net assets equals total equity, the scales balance. Fewer mistakes and faster finishes score more points.
