IB Geography Revision Guide

Condensed notes, case studies, key terms, activities and quizzes for every topic of the IB Geography course.

How to use this area

These revision guides are not intended as a substitute for your lessons in school. Use them for flip learning, consolidation and to check your understanding through formative assessment, then apply your understanding with regular IB-style question practice. Every topic brings together its lessons, with its Learning Intention, Syllabus Statement and Success criteria, clear notes organised under each criterion, case study detail, key terms, an activity, a Test My Knowledge quiz and Spaced Retrieval to keep earlier lessons fresh.
