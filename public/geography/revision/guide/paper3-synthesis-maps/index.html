16-mark essay synthesis maps

IB Geography Revision Guide · Paper 3: HL core extension · Units 4, 5 and 6 with links to the core

The 16-mark Paper 3 essay rewards synthesis: bringing ideas together from across the HL units, and the core where it helps, to build one argument and reach a judgement. Each map is built around an original practice question. Start with the four worked models: use Explore to see the ideas and how they connect, Essay route to follow one possible plan, Link challenge to practise explaining connections, and the 4Ps lens to view the ideas through places, processes, power and possibilities. Then try the Your turn essays: choose the syllabus ideas yourself, link them and plan your answer. When your teacher gives you the code, unlock the model map to compare.

What a strong 16-mark answer does

  • SynthesisesLinks at least three themes from different parts of the syllabus into one argument, rather than describing each unit in turn.
  • Uses specific evidenceNamed places, organisations, dates and figures support each point.
  • Evaluates throughoutWeighs strengths and limitations, and asks “for whom?”, “where?” and “how far?”.
  • Uses the key conceptsDraws on places, processes, power and possibilities, and links them, for example who has the power to shape a process in a place.
  • Considers perspectives and scaleCompares the views of different stakeholders, and local, national and global scales.
  • Reaches a judgementEnds with a clear, supported conclusion that answers the command term.
  • Is well structuredEach paragraph makes one point of the argument, and the paragraphs link together.

The practice questions are written for this site in an IB style. For official questions and markschemes, use your own copy of past papers.
