4. Costs, revenue and profit

Edexcel IGCSE Economics · 1.2 Business Economics · 1.2.3 Costs, revenue and profit · Lesson 4 of 10

Learning Intention

To define and calculate a firm’s costs, revenue and profit and use them to interpret a business situation.

Syllabus Statement

Definition and calculation of total revenue, total costs, total fixed costs, total variable costs, average (total) costs and profit.

Success criterion 1

Costs

  • Fixed costs (FC) do not change with output in the short run: rent, insurance, salaries of managers, loan repayments. They must be paid even if nothing is produced.
  • Variable costs (VC) change directly with output: raw materials, packaging, energy used in production, piece-rate wages.

Total cost (TC) = total fixed cost (TFC) + total variable cost (TVC)
Total variable cost = variable cost per unit × output
Average (total) cost (AC) = total cost ÷ output

Worked example: a bakery pays $2,000 rent a month. Each cake costs $3 in ingredients. If it makes 1,000 cakes: TVC = $3 × 1,000 = $3,000; TC = $2,000 + $3,000 = $5,000; AC = $5,000 ÷ 1,000 = $5 per cake.

As output rises, fixed costs are spread over more units, so average cost falls.

Success criterion 2

Revenue and profit

Total revenue (TR) = price × quantity sold
Profit = total revenue − total cost

Continuing the example: the bakery sells all 1,000 cakes at $7. TR = $7 × 1,000 = $7,000. Profit = $7,000 − $5,000 = $2,000. If total cost exceeds total revenue the firm makes a loss.

Key terms

Fixed costs
Costs that do not change with output in the short run.
Variable costs
Costs that change directly with output.
Total cost
Total fixed cost plus total variable cost.
Average cost
Total cost divided by output; the cost per unit.
Profit
Total revenue minus total cost.
Exam-style question

A firm has fixed costs of $12,000 and variable costs of $4 per unit. It sells 5,000 units at $8 each. Calculate (a) total cost, (b) average cost and (c) profit. (3 marks)

Show mark guidance
  • (a) TC = $12,000 + ($4 × 5,000) = $32,000 (1 mark).
  • (b) AC = $32,000 ÷ 5,000 = $6.40 (1 mark).
  • (c) Profit = ($8 × 5,000) − $32,000 = $40,000 − $32,000 = $8,000 (1 mark).
