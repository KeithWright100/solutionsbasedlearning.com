2.1 Government and the Economy — adapted past paper questions

45 adapted Edexcel questions (170 marks) from papers EC20J-2R, EC21-2, EC22-2, matched to the lessons. Case summaries sit with each question.

How it works

Choose a syllabus area (lesson) or a paper. For each question, type your answer, press 🎤 Speak to say it, or upload a photo of your handwritten answer. Press Submit for marking for a score and feedback based on an SBL mark scheme. Multiple-choice questions are checked instantly. Some questions use a figure or diagram from the original paper — ask your teacher for the paper that matches the code on the question (for example EC20J-2R). For drawing questions, draw on paper, then compare with the mark scheme and give yourself a mark. Your scores are saved to your account.

Questions and mark schemes are SBL’s own adaptations, written to practise the same knowledge and skills as Edexcel International GCSE Economics questions. They are not official Pearson material.
