Development and Human Welfare — adapted past paper questions

72 adapted Edexcel questions (245 marks) in the style of Paper 2, Question 9, matched to the lessons.

How it works

Choose a syllabus area (lesson) or a paper. For each question, type your answer, press 🎤 Speak to say it, or upload a photo of your handwritten answer. Press Submit for marking for a score and feedback, then compare with the mark scheme. Multiple-choice questions are checked straight away. Questions that use a figure from the Resource Booklet tell you which one — ask your teacher for the booklet that matches the code on the question (for example G24-2). Your scores are saved to your account and appear on your teacher’s class sheet.

Questions and mark schemes are SBL’s own adaptations, written to practise the same skills as Edexcel International GCSE Geography questions. They are not official Pearson material.
