Coastal Environments

Edexcel IGCSE Geography · Paper 1: Physical Geography · Section A option

About this topic

Coasts are shaped by waves, weathering and mass movement, creating distinctive landforms of erosion and deposition. They support valuable ecosystems such as sand dunes, salt marshes, mangroves and coral reefs, and they are under growing pressure from people and climate change — so they must be carefully managed.
