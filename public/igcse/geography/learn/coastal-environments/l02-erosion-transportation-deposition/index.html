Success criterion 1

The four processes of coastal erosion

ProcessWhat happens
Hydraulic actionWaves crash against the cliff, compressing air in cracks. As the wave retreats the air expands explosively, widening the cracks until pieces of rock break off.
Abrasion (corrasion)Waves throw sand and pebbles against the cliff, scraping and wearing it away like sandpaper.
AttritionRocks and pebbles carried by the waves knock into each other, becoming smaller and more rounded.
Solution (corrosion)Slightly acidic sea water dissolves soluble rocks such as chalk and limestone.

The rate of erosion is fastest where waves are powerful (destructive waves, long fetch, storms) and rocks are weak.

Success criterion 3

Longshore drift

Waves usually approach the coast at an angle, because they are driven by the prevailing wind.

  1. The swash carries sediment up the beach at the same angle as the waves.
  2. The backwash carries it straight back down the beach, at right angles to the coastline, because of gravity.
  3. This repeats again and again, moving sediment along the beach in a zig-zag.

Longshore drift supplies sediment to beaches and spits further along the coast. Groynes are built to trap it.

Success criterion 4

What is coastal deposition?

Deposition is when the sea drops the material it is carrying, because it no longer has enough energy to transport it. It happens:

  • in sheltered bays, where wave energy is spread out;
  • where the coastline changes direction and the water becomes calmer;
  • when constructive waves dominate (strong swash, weak backwash);
  • where there is a large supply of sediment, e.g. from eroding cliffs or rivers.

Deposition creates beaches, spits, bars and tombolos (Lesson 7).

Key terms

Hydraulic action
Erosion by the force of waves compressing air in cracks.
Abrasion
Erosion by waves throwing sediment against the cliff.
Attrition
Sediment knocking together and becoming smaller and rounder.
Longshore drift
The zig-zag movement of sediment along a coast by swash and backwash.
Deposition
When the sea drops material because it has lost energy.
