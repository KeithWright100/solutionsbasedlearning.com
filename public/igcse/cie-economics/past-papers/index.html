Specimen paper practice

The Cambridge IGCSE Economics 0455 specimen papers (for examination from 2027): 40 multiple-choice questions and the full Paper 2, matched to the revision topics.

How it works

Choose a paper or a syllabus area. Multiple-choice questions are checked instantly. For written answers, type your answer, press 🎤 Speak to say it, or upload a photo of your handwritten answer, then press Submit for marking for a score and feedback based on the official mark scheme. For the diagram question, draw on paper, compare with the mark scheme and give yourself a mark. In Paper 2 you answer Question 1 and any three of Questions 2–5. Your scores are saved to your account.
