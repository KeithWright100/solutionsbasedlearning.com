Cambridge IGCSE Economics (0455)

Topic-by-topic revision for the Cambridge syllabus, for examination from 2027: notes, diagrams, key terms, activities, quizzes and exam practice.

How to use this area

This area follows the Cambridge IGCSE Economics 0455 syllabus and is separate from the Edexcel IGCSE lessons. It is not a substitute for your lessons in school. Use it for flip learning, consolidation and to check your understanding. The syllabus has six topics, and each one is split into a few revision chunks. Every chunk has its Learning Intention, Syllabus Statement and Success criteria, clear notes organised under each criterion, labelled diagrams, key terms, an activity, Paper 2-style questions with mark guidance, a Test My Knowledge quiz and Spaced Retrieval.

The exam

PaperTime and marksWhat you do
Paper 1 Multiple choice1 hour, 40 marks (30%)40 multiple-choice questions on the whole syllabus.
Paper 2 Structured questions2 hours, 80 marks (70%)Section A: one compulsory 20-mark data response question. Section B: answer three questions from four, each 20 marks: (a) 2, (b) 4, (c) 6 and (d) 8 marks.

Command words matter: define and identify need short, accurate answers; explain needs a point plus why or how; analyse needs a developed chain of reasoning; discuss needs both sides of the argument, developed, with a reasoned conclusion.
