10. Calculating and analysing accounting ratios

Edexcel IGCSE Business · 3 Business finance · 3.5 Accounts analysis · Lesson 10 of 11

Learning Intention

To calculate and analyse profitability and liquidity ratios.

Syllabus Statement

Calculating and analysing accounting ratios: gross profit margin, operating profit margin, markup, return on capital employed (ROCE), current ratio, acid test ratio. (Formulae are provided in the examination.)

Success criterion 1

Profitability ratios

Ratios turn figures into percentages or proportions so that performance can be compared. The formulae are given in the exam.

Gross profit margin = (gross profit ÷ revenue) × 100

Operating profit margin = (operating profit ÷ revenue) × 100

Markup = (profit per item ÷ cost per item) × 100

Worked example: Lotus Café

Revenue $200,000; gross profit $80,000; operating profit $30,000.

  • Gross profit margin = 80,000 ÷ 200,000 × 100 = 40%
  • Operating profit margin = 30,000 ÷ 200,000 × 100 = 15%

A cake costs $2.50 to make and sells for $4.00. Profit per item = $1.50. Markup = 1.50 ÷ 2.50 × 100 = 60%.

Success criterion 2

Return on capital employed

ROCE = (operating profit ÷ capital employed) × 100

ROCE shows how well the business uses its long-term finance to make profit. Lotus Café: 30,000 ÷ 170,000 × 100 = 17.6%. Investors compare ROCE with the interest they could earn in a bank: the higher the ROCE, the better.

Success criterion 3

Liquidity ratios

Current ratio = current assets ÷ current liabilities

Acid test ratio = (current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities

Worked example

Current assets $60,000 (including inventory $25,000); current liabilities $40,000.

  • Current ratio = 60,000 ÷ 40,000 = 1.5 (often written 1.5:1)
  • Acid test = (60,000 − 25,000) ÷ 40,000 = 0.875

The acid test removes inventory because stock may be hard to sell quickly. You will interpret these ratios in the next lesson.

Try it

Revenue $500,000; gross profit $150,000; operating profit $40,000; capital employed $320,000. Calculate the gross profit margin, operating profit margin and ROCE.

Show answer

GPM = 150,000 ÷ 500,000 × 100 = 30%. OPM = 40,000 ÷ 500,000 × 100 = 8%. ROCE = 40,000 ÷ 320,000 × 100 = 12.5%.

Key terms

Gross profit margin
Gross profit as a percentage of revenue.
Operating profit margin
Operating profit as a percentage of revenue.
Markup
Profit per item as a percentage of the cost per item.
ROCE
Operating profit as a percentage of capital employed.
Acid test ratio
(Current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities.
Exam-style question

A business has current assets of $84,000, of which $36,000 is inventory. Its current liabilities are $60,000. Calculate the acid test ratio. (2 marks)

Show mark guidance
  • 1 mark: current assets − inventory = 84,000 − 36,000 = $48,000.
  • 1 mark: acid test = 48,000 ÷ 60,000 = 0.8 (0.8:1).

Model answer: Current assets minus inventory = 84,000 − 36,000 = $48,000. Acid test ratio = 48,000 ÷ 60,000 = 0.8.
