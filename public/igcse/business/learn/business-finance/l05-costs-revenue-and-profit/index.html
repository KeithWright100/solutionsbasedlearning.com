5. Costs, revenue and profit

Edexcel IGCSE Business · 3 Business finance · 3.3 Costs and break-even analysis · Lesson 5 of 11

Learning Intention

To calculate revenue, fixed, variable and total costs, and profit or loss.

Syllabus Statement

The concept and calculation of: revenue; fixed and variable costs; total costs; profit and loss.

Success criteria

I can…

Tick each one when you are confident. Your ticks are saved to your account.

Success criterion 1

Revenue

Revenue = selling price × quantity sold

A bakery sells 2,000 loaves at $3 each. Revenue = 3 × 2,000 = $6,000.

Success criterion 2

Fixed, variable and total costs

Fixed costs do not change with the level of output in the short run: rent, salaries, insurance, loan interest, advertising. They must be paid even if nothing is produced.

Variable costs change directly with output: raw materials, packaging, piece-rate wages, energy used in production.

Total variable costs = variable cost per unit × quantity

Total costs = fixed costs + total variable costs

Activity: fixed or variable?

Classify each cost for a T-shirt printing business.

  • Rent of the workshop
  • Blank T-shirts
  • Manager’s salary
  • Ink used for printing
  • Insurance
  • Packaging for each order

Success criterion 3

Profit and loss

Profit (or loss) = revenue − total costs

Worked example

The bakery’s fixed costs are $2,000 a month and its variable cost is $1.20 per loaf. It sells 2,000 loaves at $3.

  • Revenue = 3 × 2,000 = $6,000
  • Total variable costs = 1.20 × 2,000 = $2,400
  • Total costs = 2,000 + 2,400 = $4,400
  • Profit = 6,000 − 4,400 = $1,600

If total costs are greater than revenue, the result is negative: a loss.

Try it

A business sells 500 units at $20. Fixed costs are $4,000 and variable cost is $14 per unit. Calculate the profit or loss.

Show answer

Revenue = $10,000. TVC = 14 × 500 = $7,000. TC = 4,000 + 7,000 = $11,000. Loss = 10,000 − 11,000 = ($1,000).

Key terms

Revenue
Money received from sales: price × quantity sold.
Fixed costs
Costs that do not change with the level of output.
Variable costs
Costs that change directly with the level of output.
Total costs
Fixed costs plus total variable costs.
Loss
When total costs are greater than revenue.
Exam-style question

A business sells 1,200 units at $15. Fixed costs are $6,000 and the variable cost per unit is $8. Calculate the profit. (3 marks)

Show mark guidance
  • 1 mark: revenue = 15 × 1,200 = $18,000.
  • 1 mark: total costs = 6,000 + (8 × 1,200) = 6,000 + 9,600 = $15,600.
  • 1 mark: profit = 18,000 − 15,600 = $2,400.

Model answer: Revenue = 15 × 1,200 = $18,000. Total variable costs = 8 × 1,200 = $9,600. Total costs = 6,000 + 9,600 = $15,600. Profit = 18,000 − 15,600 = $2,400.
