1. The purpose of market research

Edexcel IGCSE Business · 4 Marketing · 4.1 Market research · Lesson 1 of 11

Learning Intention

To understand why businesses carry out market research.

Syllabus Statement

The purpose of market research: to identify and understand customer needs; to identify gaps in the market; to reduce risk; to inform business decisions.

Success criterion 1

What is market research?

Market research is the process of collecting, recording and analysing information about customers, competitors and the market. It helps a business find out what customers want, how much they will pay, and what competitors are doing.

Success criterion 2

Why businesses do market research

PurposeExplanation
Identify and understand customer needsFind out what customers want, how much they will pay and where they shop, so products meet their needs
Identify gaps in the marketSpot a need that no business is meeting, e.g. no vegan café in a town
Reduce riskTesting an idea before spending large sums makes failure less likely
Inform business decisionsEvidence helps decide prices, product features, promotion and location

Activity: which purpose?

Match each example to the main purpose of the research.

  • Asking customers which flavours they prefer
  • Discovering there is no gym in a new housing estate
  • Test marketing a drink in one city before a national launch
  • Finding out what price parents will pay for school shoes
  • Finding that no local shop sells halal ready meals
  • Surveying 500 people before investing $1 million

Success criterion 3

Market research and decisions

Research gives managers evidence instead of guesses. For example, research might show that young customers want a phone app for ordering, leading a restaurant to invest in one. However, research costs money and time, and if it is poorly designed or out of date it can lead to the wrong decisions.

Key terms

Market research
Collecting and analysing information about customers, competitors and markets.
Customer needs
What customers want from a product or service.
Gap in the market
A customer need that no business is currently meeting.
Risk
The chance that a decision leads to a loss or failure.
Competitor
A business selling similar products to the same customers.
Exam-style question

Explain one reason why a business carries out market research before launching a new product. (3 marks)

Show mark guidance
  • 1 mark for a reason: e.g. to reduce risk.
  • 1 mark for development: e.g. it finds out whether customers want the product and what they would pay.
  • 1 mark for consequence: e.g. so the business avoids spending money on a product that will not sell.

Model answer: Market research reduces risk. By asking potential customers whether they would buy the product and at what price, the business can find out if there is enough demand before spending money on production and promotion, so it is less likely to launch a product that fails.
