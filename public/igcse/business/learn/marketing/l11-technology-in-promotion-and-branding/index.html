11. Technology in promotion and the importance of a brand

Edexcel IGCSE Business · 4 Marketing · 4.3 The marketing mix · Lesson 11 of 11

Learning Intention

To understand how technology is used in promotion and why brands matter.

Syllabus Statement

Promotion: the use of technology in promotion – targeted advertising online, viral advertising via social media, e-newsletters; the importance of a brand.

Success criterion 3

The importance of a brand

A brand is a name, logo, design or image that identifies a business or product and makes it different from competitors.

  • Customers recognise and trust strong brands, so new products are easier to launch.
  • Brands build loyalty: customers keep buying and recommend them.
  • Brands allow a premium price, increasing profit margins.
  • A strong brand makes it harder for competitors to win customers.

However, brands take time and money to build, and a single scandal can damage a brand’s reputation quickly.

Key terms

Targeted advertising
Online advertising shown to people based on their data, such as interests and location.
Viral advertising
Content that spreads rapidly as people share it on social media.
E-newsletter
A regular email sent to customers with news and offers.
Brand
A name, logo or image that identifies a product and distinguishes it from competitors.
Brand loyalty
When customers repeatedly choose the same brand.
Exam-style question

Discuss whether a new fashion brand should spend its promotion budget on social media rather than traditional advertising. (6 marks)

Show mark guidance
  • Up to 2 marks for knowledge of social media / traditional methods.
  • Up to 2 marks for analysis: e.g. social media is cheap, targeted at young fashion buyers and can go viral; but negative comments spread and it is crowded.
  • Up to 2 marks for a justified judgement.

Model answer: Social media would allow the brand to target young people interested in fashion precisely, at a much lower cost than TV or magazine adverts, and posts could be shared widely, giving huge reach for little money. Results are also easy to measure. However, social media is crowded with competitors, and negative comments can spread quickly. Traditional advertising can build credibility but is expensive. Because the target market spends a lot of time online and the budget is limited, social media is the better choice, perhaps working with influencers.
