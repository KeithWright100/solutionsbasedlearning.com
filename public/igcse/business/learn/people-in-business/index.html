2 People in business

Edexcel IGCSE Business · The specification: five topics · 10 lessons

About this topic

This topic is about the people who make a business work: how they communicate, how they are recruited, selected and trained, the laws that protect them, what motivates them, and how they are organised into structures and functional departments.
