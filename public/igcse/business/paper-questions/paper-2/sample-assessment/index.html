How it works

Choose a syllabus area (lesson) or a paper. For each question, type your answer, press 🎤 Speak to say it, or upload a photo of your handwritten answer. Press Submit for marking for a score and feedback based on the official mark scheme. Multiple-choice questions are checked instantly. For drawing questions, draw on paper, then compare with the mark scheme and give yourself a mark. Your scores are saved to your account.
