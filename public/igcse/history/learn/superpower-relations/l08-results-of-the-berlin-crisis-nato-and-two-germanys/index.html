8. Results of the Berlin Crisis: two Germanys and NATO

Edexcel IGCSE History · A world divided: superpower relations, 1943–72 · 2. Early developments in the Cold War, 1945–49 · Lesson 8 of 19

Learning Intention

To understand how the Berlin Crisis led to two German states and NATO.

Syllabus Statement

Results of the Berlin Crisis (1948–49), including the setting up of NATO and the creation of two Germanys: the Federal Republic (FRG) and the Democratic Republic (GDR).

Success criterion 1

Two Germanys, 1949

DateEvent
8 May 1949Just days after the blockade ended, the USA, Britain and France allow their zones to join as the Federal Republic of Germany (FRG)
14 August 1949West Germans elect their own parliament, the Bundestag
15 September 1949Konrad Adenauer becomes the first democratically elected chancellor; the capital is Bonn
October 1949Stalin creates the German Democratic Republic (GDR) in the Soviet zone

The Western zones of Berlin became West Berlin. Only communist countries recognised the GDR, and the FRG refused to accept that Germany had been split until the 1970s. For the next 40 years people spoke of West and East Germany.

Success criterion 2

The creation of NATO, April 1949

Stalin’s threat to Berlin and the communist takeover in Czechoslovakia in 1948 persuaded the West it needed a formal military alliance. In April 1949 the USA, Britain, France and nine other countries formed the North Atlantic Treaty Organization (NATO). Article 5 said an armed attack against one member would be considered an attack against them all. British foreign secretary Ernest Bevin played a major part in bringing it about.

Success criterion 3

Why NATO mattered

  • It was a major step beyond the Truman Doctrine: the USA was now formally committed to the defence of Western Europe.
  • The Americans kept bases in Europe throughout the Cold War.
  • It sent Stalin a message about the West’s determination to stand firm against communism.
  • Europe was now divided politically, economically and militarily. When West Germany joined NATO in 1955, the Soviet Union responded with the Warsaw Pact.

Key terms

FRG
The Federal Republic of Germany (West Germany), founded 1949.
GDR
The German Democratic Republic (East Germany), founded 1949.
NATO
The North Atlantic Treaty Organization, a Western military alliance formed in 1949.
Article 5
The NATO promise that an attack on one member is an attack on all.
Bundestag
The parliament of West Germany.
