A world divided: superpower relations, 1943–72

Edexcel IGCSE History · Paper 1: Depth studies · 19 lessons

About this topic

This depth study follows how the wartime Grand Alliance broke down into a Cold War: the conferences of 1943–45, the division of Europe and Germany, the arms race and the crises of the 1950s and 1960s, and the moves towards détente by 1972. Work through the lessons in order. Each lesson starts with its Learning Intention, Syllabus Statement and Success criteria; the notes are organised under each success criterion.
