A divided union: civil rights in the USA, 1945–74

Edexcel IGCSE History · Paper 1: Depth studies · 25 lessons

About this topic

This depth study follows the Red Scare and McCarthyism, the struggle against segregation in the 1950s, the protests, laws and Black Power of the 1960s, the student, anti-war and women’s movements, and the Watergate scandal that ended Nixon’s presidency. Work through the lessons in order. Each lesson starts with its Learning Intention, Syllabus Statement and Success criteria; the notes are organised under each success criterion. Tick the criteria off as you go, then use Test My Knowledge and Spaced Retrieval to check your learning.
