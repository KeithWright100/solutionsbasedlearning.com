15. The Civil War, 1918–20

Success criterion 1

Causes and sides

Opponents of the Bolsheviks included monarchists, upper- and middle-class people, supporters of the Constituent Assembly, and nationalities wanting independence. Brest-Litovsk united many of them.

SideKey features
RedsThe Bolsheviks. Trotsky, as war commissar, built the Red Army with ex-tsarist officers watched by political commissars; deserters were executed. Conscription from May 1918; 5.4 million men by 1921.
WhitesDifferent groups united only against the Bolsheviks: SRs, monarchists and army officers. Main armies: Kolchak in Siberia, Yudenich from Estonia, Denikin (then Wrangel) in the south, and the People’s Army of Komuch in Samara.
GreensPeasant armies defending their own areas, such as the Ukrainian anarchist Nestor Makhno.
Foreign powersBritain sent supplies worth £100 million; Japan sent 70,000 troops to Siberia and the USA followed; the 50,000-strong Czech Legion seized much of the Trans-Siberian Railway.
Success criterion 2

Key events

DateEvent
3 March 1918Brest-Litovsk unites opposition at home and abroad.
13 March 1918Trotsky becomes war commissar.
May 1918The Czech Legion refuses to disarm and joins the SRs.
17 July 1918Nicholas II and his family are shot at Yekaterinburg as White forces approach.
30 August 1918Fanya Kaplan shoots Lenin, leading to the Red Terror.
November 1918–June 1919Kolchak advances to within 800 km of Moscow, then is forced back.
October 1919Yudenich nearly reaches Petrograd; Denikin gets within about 520 km of Moscow; both are driven back.
August 1920Tambov uprisings against grain taxes; 70,000 peasants fight by February 1921.
7–15 November 1920The Red Army defeats Wrangel at Perekop, the final battle.
Success criterion 3

The cost

At least 1 million people died in combat or from Red or White terror; several million more died from disease, hunger and cold, and about a million left the country. After the Whites were defeated, the Reds sent 100,000 troops into Tambov and used poison gas on peasants hiding in forests.

Key terms

Reds
The Bolsheviks and their Red Army.
Whites
The different groups fighting to remove the Bolsheviks.
Greens
Peasant armies fighting to control their own local areas.
Czech Legion
A 50,000-strong force that seized much of the Trans-Siberian Railway.
Commissar
A Bolshevik official, such as those supervising Red Army officers.
