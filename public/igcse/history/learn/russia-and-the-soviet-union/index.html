Edexcel IGCSE History · Paper 2: Investigation and breadth studies · 20 lessons

About this topic

This investigation follows the end of tsarist rule, from the 1905 revolution and the dumas through the First World War and the February Revolution, to the Bolshevik takeover, the Civil War, War Communism, the NEP and Lenin’s achievements by 1924. Work through the lessons in order. Each lesson starts with its Learning Intention, Syllabus Statement and Success criteria; the notes are organised under each success criterion. Tick the criteria off as you go, then use Test My Knowledge and Spaced Retrieval to check your learning.
