7. The tsarina and the influence of Rasputin

Edexcel IGCSE History · Russia and the Soviet Union, 1905–24 · 2. Opposition to tsarist rule, 1914–17 · Lesson 7 of 20

Learning Intention

To understand why the tsarina became regent and how Rasputin’s influence increased opposition to tsarism.

Syllabus Statement

The influence of Rasputin.

Success criterion 1

The tsarina in control

When Nicholas went to army headquarters, 800 km from Petrograd (St Petersburg was renamed because it sounded German), he left his wife Alexandra as regent.

  • She was devoted to autocracy and refused to deal with the duma. Between August 1915 and December 1916 she appointed four prime ministers and three ministers of war.
  • She was German, which made her deeply unpopular.
  • She became obsessed with Grigori Rasputin, a Siberian peasant-monk said to have mystic healing powers.
Success criterion 2

Rasputin’s influence

Rasputin believed the way to God was by sinning and then repenting, and was accused of drinking, stealing and affairs. The heir, Alexei, had haemophilia, so any injury could be fatal. When Rasputin seemed able to help him, Alexandra came to depend on him. She took his advice, appointed ministers he recommended, and even wrote to Nicholas about Rasputin’s dreams and what they meant for the war, such as urging an advance near Riga in November 1915.

Success criterion 3

Opposition and murder

Rasputin was hated by the court and government. Cartoons showed him as the real ruler with Nicholas and Alexandra as his puppets. Rumours spread that he was having an affair with Alexandra, and that she was betraying military secrets to Germany. These rumours so damaged the ruling class that in December 1916 a group of aristocrats murdered him. Alexandra was upset, but there was public rejoicing.

Key terms

Regent
A person appointed to govern because the ruler is away, ill or too young.
Petrograd
The new, more Russian name for St Petersburg from 1914.
Haemophilia
A disease in which the blood does not clot properly.
Rasputin
A Siberian peasant-monk who gained great influence over Tsarina Alexandra.
Repenting
Deeply regretting something you have done wrong.
