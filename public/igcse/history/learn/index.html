Edexcel IGCSE History

Lessons, timelines, activities and quizzes for the Edexcel International GCSE in History.

How to use this area

These lessons are not intended as a substitute for your lessons in school. Use for flip learning, consolidation, to check your understanding through formative assessment and conducting regular Past Paper Practice to apply your understanding. Every lesson has clear notes, key people and events, timelines, key terms, activities, a Test My Knowledge quiz and Spaced Retrieval to keep earlier lessons fresh. When you are ready, read the exam tips to plan your essay answers.
