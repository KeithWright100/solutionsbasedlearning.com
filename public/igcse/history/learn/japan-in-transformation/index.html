Japan in transformation, 1853–1945

Edexcel IGCSE History · Paper 2: Investigation and breadth studies · 15 lessons

About this topic

This breadth study follows Japan from the end of its isolation in 1853 and the fall of the shogunate, through its rise as an imperial power and its wars with China and Russia, to party government, the rise of militarism and defeat in the Second World War. Work through the lessons in order. Each lesson starts with its Learning Intention, Syllabus Statement and Success criteria; the notes are organised under each success criterion.
