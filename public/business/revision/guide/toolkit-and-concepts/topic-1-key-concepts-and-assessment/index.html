1. The key concepts and how the course is assessed

Success criterion 1

The four key concepts

ConceptMeaningBusiness examples
ChangeBusinesses must adapt to changes in technology, markets, laws, the economy and society; change brings opportunities and resistance.Moving to e-commerce; restructuring; responding to a pandemic
CreativityGenerating new ideas, products, processes and ways of solving problems; links to innovation and R&D.New product design; creative marketing; new business models
EthicsMoral principles that guide decisions: doing what is right, not only what is legal or profitable.Fair pay in supply chains; honest advertising; data privacy
SustainabilityMeeting present needs without compromising future generations: economic, social and environmental.Renewable energy; circular design; long-term profitability

Concepts are “lenses” that connect topics across the course. In extended answers, use them to deepen your evaluation, e.g. “Although outsourcing cuts costs, it raises ethical questions about working conditions and may not be sustainable if…”.

Success criterion 3

Command terms

Assessment objectiveCommand terms
AO1: knowledge and understandingDefine, describe, identify, list, outline, state
AO2: application and analysisAnalyse, apply, comment, demonstrate, distinguish, explain, suggest
AO3: synthesis and evaluationCompare (and contrast), discuss, evaluate, examine, justify, recommend, to what extent
AO4: selecting and using skillsAnnotate, calculate, complete, construct, determine, draw, label, plot, prepare

Exam tip: Do not answer AO2 and AO3 questions in bullet points: examiners need developed, linked explanations and a justified judgement. For calculations, always show your working and units.

Success criterion 4

The internal assessment: business research project

  • Choose a real business issue or problem facing one organisation, related to any part of the syllabus.
  • Write a research question (it can look backwards or forwards).
  • Use one key concept (change, creativity, ethics or sustainability) as a lens throughout, and state it on the title page.
  • Base the project on 3–5 supporting documents (primary and/or secondary research) chosen for relevance and depth.
  • Apply relevant business management tools and theories to analyse the issue.
  • Write no more than 1,800 words, reference all sources and include a bibliography.
  • Around 20 hours is recommended; marked by your teacher and moderated by the IB.

Key terms

Change
The key concept of adapting to new circumstances.
Creativity
The key concept of generating new ideas and solutions.
Ethics
The key concept of moral principles guiding business decisions.
Sustainability
The key concept of meeting present needs without harming future generations.
Command term
A word in a question that tells you what kind of answer is required.
Supporting documents
The 3–5 sources on which the IA research is based.
