A four-part (quarterly) moving average needs centring, because the average of four quarters falls between two quarters: take the average of two consecutive four-quarter averages.

Success criterion 2

HLSeasonal variation and forecasting

Seasonal variation = actual sales − trend (moving average). The average seasonal variation for each season smooths out random variations. To forecast: extend (extrapolate) the trend line, then add the average seasonal variation for that season.

Sales data can also show cyclical variation (linked to the business cycle) and random variation (unpredictable events such as a pandemic or a viral social media post).

Key terms

Sales forecasting
HL: predicting future sales.
Moving average
HL: an average of a set number of consecutive data points, used to show the trend.
Trend
HL: the underlying direction of the data.
Seasonal variation
HL: the difference between actual sales and the trend for a season.
Extrapolation
HL: extending a trend line to forecast future values.
Exam-style question

Explain two limitations of using moving averages to forecast sales for a fashion retailer. (4 marks)

Show mark guidance
  • Up to 4 marks: two developed points (or one fully developed point for a 2-mark question), each explained and applied to the case.
  • Use the command term: "explain" needs cause and effect, not a list.
  • e.g. based on past data while fashion changes fast; ignores random shocks; trend may change.

Model answer: Fashion tastes change quickly, so past sales patterns may not continue and the extrapolated trend could be wrong. Moving averages also smooth out sudden events such as a viral trend or a supply problem, so they cannot predict them.
