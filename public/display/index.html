Display mode

Display mode shows SBL pages one after another on a big screen, for open days and conferences. Log in with a teacher account to set it up.

Display mode

Choose the pages to show, then press Start. The screen goes full screen and moves to the next page every few seconds. Press Esc or Stop to switch it off. Your choices are saved on this computer.
